Ice Warriors

Welton Waters · It's never too cold

About

Ice Warriors swim together in the open water at Welton Waters Adventure Centre.

Where

Welton Waters Adventure Centre.

When

Wednesday evenings from May through to September (weather permitting). You can arrive anytime between 5pm and 7pm, but last entry to the water is 6.30pm. Follow our Instagram or join our Facebook group for updates about sessions.

Come along

Fill in the online form, then just turn up with your equipment and a £5 note.

Safety

Please ensure you have a bright coloured swim cap and tow float. These can be purchased from us if you need one. We have an incredible team of volunteers on and off the water who are there to help you.
