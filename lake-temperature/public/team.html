Meet the team

Andy Butler

Chief and founder

Andy is the chief and founder of Ice Warriors. He has a wide range of open water qualifications and experience, from swimming an Ice Kilometre to an annual skinny dip! He's usually found on the platform waiting to give advice to anyone who requests it. You can approach him, he'll be happy to help.

Eileen Butler

On the platform

Eileen is Andy's much better half. She welcomes swimmers as they arrive on the platform with a friendly smile, then she'll take your money! Your entry fee anyway, and get you signed up if you're new to Ice Warriors.

Gaye Hanson

Platform co-ordinator

Gaye is our platform co-ordinator, keeping us all in check (yes, swimmers and volunteers!) and making sure week on week that everyone is safe. She organises the volunteers and checks that each swimmer getting in the water has the correct safety attire.

Leanne Smith

Social media

Leanne looks after the group's social media as well as volunteers on and off the water. Sometimes you'll find her on the platform either gabbing or gathering content, other times you'll find her on the water also gabbing or gathering content!

Martin John Shepherd

Safety co-ordinator

Martin is our safety co-ordinator, often found around the platform area waiting to jump in the safety boat to rescue anyone who needs it (which thankfully is a rarity, so he is also usually found gabbing!).
